Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SoundDry handles user privacy.

Data We Collect

SoundDry does not require personal information in order to use its core speaker cleaning and sound frequency features.

The app does not collect or store directly identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or similar personal information on its own servers.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. Payment and billing information is handled by Apple. SoundDry does not have access to this payment information.

Device Features

The app may generate specific sound frequencies through your device speaker for speaker cleaning purposes. No personal data is collected during this process.

Third-Party Services

If the app uses third-party services such as analytics or crash reporting in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
